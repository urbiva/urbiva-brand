# Typography

| Role | Typeface | Styles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wordmark | Asimovian | Outlined in logo files; never typeset in UI |
| Display and headings | Manrope | ExtraBold 40, Bold 28, Bold 22, SemiBold 18 |
| Body and labels | Inter | Regular 16, 14, 12; SemiBold 14, 12 |
| Data, ids, overlines | IBM Plex Mono | Medium 13, Regular 11, SemiBold 11 uppercase |

## Azerbaijani

Azerbaijani is the primary product language. Test every specimen with `Ç Ə Ğ I İ Ö Ş Ü` and their lower-case forms. Manrope has no capital Ə (U+018F); the app font stack falls back to Inter for that glyph, and the Figma files apply Inter to it explicitly.
